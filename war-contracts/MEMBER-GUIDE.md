# War Contracts — member setup and announcement

## Ready-to-copy faction announcement

War Contracts is available through Script Hub. It finds suitable med-out partners using your own stats/gear and FFScouter public estimates, and hides players already paired or being contacted.

1. Install/update [Script Hub](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/600221-torn-script-hub) and [War Contracts](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/600540-torn-war-contracts-hub). Keep one enabled copy of each.
2. In Hub API Keys, approve your own Torn key for War Contracts. Register your estimate key with FFScouter first. Keys stay private; never put one in chat or sheet notes.
3. Open Hub → War Contracts → Settings → Connection settings. Paste the faction service address supplied by leadership once, then Save and reconnect.
4. Contracts shows existing manual/script agreements. If unpaired, use Matches → Find my matches, choose your planned weapon and review a partner. Recommendations assume the opponent strips; check their estimate and agree on terms.
5. Reserve and prepare message, edit/copy the invitation and send it yourself. Mark Message sent. Once both players agree, review the hits, time in TCT, Warlord and notes; confirm stripping and the final preview to save.
6. My Deal shows your booking. Release an abandoned pending invitation, or use Cancel deal to explicitly cancel your own confirmed/manual agreement. Tell your opponent yourself. Cancelled history stays in the sheet.

If a save/cancellation times out, use Check saved state before trying again. Never send a duplicate invitation because a request was slow. Estimated respect excludes chain/retaliation/group/overseas modifiers and fixed bonus hits; match fit is not a guaranteed win. Ended wars are read-only.

## Leadership before distributing

- Share your live faction /exec service address separately; the spreadsheet link is not the endpoint. Do not publish credentials.
- Confirm the sheet hit cap and current war details. Existing values/formulas are preserved; genuinely blank start/opponent headers fill for scheduled/current wars.
- On each new war, archive/review and clear previous completed pairings/obsolete terms before reusing the manual table. The service guards same-opponent rematches too.
- Desktop and responsive/native-bridge fixture checks are separate from testing an actual Torn PDA device. Verify a phone installation before describing native phone support as fully checked.
